Local Attendance is a privacy-first, fully on-device facial recognition attendance application designed for educators, schools, and organizations.

Key Features:

• 100% On-Device & Private: Face detection and ArcFace neural network embeddings run entirely locally on your device hardware. Zero photos or biometric data ever leave the device or touch external servers.

• Flexible Attendance Modes:
  - Single Mode (Front Camera): Fast 1-on-1 verification with geometric liveness and anti-spoofing verification.
  - Classroom Mode (Back Camera): Identifies multiple students simultaneously across the room in real time.

• Privacy-Preserving Vector Import: In addition to standard photo ZIP archives, enroll classes securely using pre-computed ArcFace vector embeddings without needing to handle raw student photos on the device.

• Complete Attendance Workflow: Create classes, manage student rosters, schedule sessions, mark attendance with visual feedback, and export attendance records directly to CSV or JSON.

• Zero Telemetry & Offline-First: No Firebase, no analytics, and no tracking libraries. Operates completely offline without internet connectivity.
