Privacy Policy

Local Attendance • Last updated: October 29, 2026

Local Attendance is an offline-first facial recognition attendance management system developed by Evan Ferrao.

We believe privacy is an uncompromising requirement, particularly when handling educational and biometric data. Unlike traditional attendance platforms that upload employee and student photos to cloud databases, Local Attendance is engineered from the ground up to operate 100% locally on-device without cloud connectivity.

Core Principle: Your biometric data, student records, and attendance logs never leave your physical device. We operate zero cloud servers, maintain zero remote databases, and possess zero access to your data.

1. Information Processed Locally

All data created, processed, or imported within the application is stored strictly within the app's private sandbox storage on your local hardware:

  • Facial Biometric Vectors: When enrolling students or taking attendance, the application processes facial images through local on-device machine learning models to generate mathematical feature vectors (embeddings). Raw facial photos are never transmitted over any network.
  • Roster Information: Student and teacher names, identification numbers / roll codes, departmental categories, and optional local reference images.
  • Attendance Records: Timestamps, class session names, verification confidence scores, and attendance statuses (present, absent, late).

2. On-Device Machine Learning (Zero Cloud Inference)

All computer vision computation is performed entirely on your device's local processor:

  • Google ML Kit Vision: Runs on-device face detection to calculate facial bounding boxes and landmarks.
  • ONNX Runtime: Executes quantized neural network models (MobileFaceNet) on local CPU/GPU hardware to extract feature vectors.

The inference pipeline requires no internet access, and no biometric payloads are ever broadcast externally.

3. Device Permissions & Usage

The application requests only two device permissions, strictly required to deliver offline functionality:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Required to scan student faces during live attendance acquisition and capture reference photos during student enrollment. Video frames are analyzed in temporary RAM and are instantly discarded.
  • Storage Access: Used solely when the user explicitly triggers local data import (such as importing a student ZIP archive) or local data export (saving attendance reports in CSV, Excel, or PDF formats).

4. Zero Third-Party Trackers & Advertising

Local Attendance respects your digital sovereignty:

  • No third-party analytics SDKs or behavioral trackers.
  • No advertising networks or Google Advertising ID (GAID) collection.
  • No telemetry or remote error-monitoring beacons.
  • We do not collect, buy, sell, rent, or trade any personal data.

5. Data Retention, Control & Deletion

Because all database records reside exclusively in your local device SQLite storage, you maintain total ownership:

  • In-App Management: You can edit or permanently delete individual students, courses, or session history at any time from within the application.
  • Instant Complete Wipe: Clearing app data via Android system settings (Settings > Apps > Local Attendance > Storage > Clear Storage) or uninstalling the application instantly and irrecoverably erases all databases, models, and biometric vector caches from your device.

6. Educational & Children's Privacy Compliance

Because Local Attendance never collects, stores, or transmits data to remote servers, it inherently complies with educational privacy standards, including the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Institutions retain sole physical custody of their records.
