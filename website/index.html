Privacy Policy

Local Attendance is built on a strict offline-first, zero-cloud architecture. Your biometric facial vectors, attendance logs, and student records never leave your physical device.

🛡️
100% On-Device
All facial recognition inference runs locally using embedded ONNX neural models and Google ML Kit. No cloud servers are involved.
📷
Ephemeral Camera
Camera frames are processed in temporary volatile memory to extract math embeddings and are immediately discarded.
🚫
No Trackers or Ads
We do not integrate advertising networks, tracking cookies, or third-party behavioral analytics SDKs.
📁
User-Owned Data
All student rosters, session records, and logs reside solely in your local SQLite database. You retain 100% control over exports.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Local Attendance ("we", "our", or "the Application"), developed by Evan Ferrao, handles your information.

We believe privacy is a fundamental human right. Unlike conventional cloud-based attendance solutions that upload sensitive biometric data to external servers, Local Attendance is explicitly designed as a local-first, fully offline application.

2. Information Processed & Stored Locally

All data created or managed within the Application is stored solely on your device's internal storage:

  • Facial Biometric Embeddings: When registering a student or taking attendance, the Application processes facial images through local machine learning models to extract a non-reversible numerical vector (embedding). Raw facial images are not transmitted anywhere.
  • Student & Teacher Records: Name, Student ID / Roll Number, Department, Section, and optionally a local reference photo.
  • Attendance Sessions: Timestamps, class name, date, present/absent status, and verification confidence scores.
Key Guarantee: No personal or biometric data is ever uploaded to, synchronized with, or backed up to any cloud server operated by us or third parties.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

The Application requests the following Android permissions strictly to fulfill its core educational functionality:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used exclusively during live attendance scanning sessions and when capturing reference photos for student registration. The camera stream is analyzed in real-time within device RAM and is never recorded, streamed, or broadcast.
  • Storage / File Access (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / Media Picker): Used exclusively when you deliberately choose to: (a) Import a batch ZIP archive of student photos from your device, or (b) Export generated attendance reports (in CSV, Excel, or PDF formats) to your local storage or share them via the standard Android share sheet.

4. On-Device Machine Learning Architecture

Face detection and face recognition are powered by on-device inference engines:

  • Google ML Kit Vision: Operates entirely on-device to locate facial bounding boxes and landmarks.
  • ONNX Runtime: Executes quantized lightweight neural network models (such as MobileFaceNet) locally on your device's CPU/GPU to generate mathematical embeddings.

At no point are model weights, camera frames, or feature vectors exchanged with external endpoints.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all data is stored exclusively on your device within the Application's private SQLite sandbox:

  • You have full control: You may edit or delete individual student profiles, attendance sessions, or teacher accounts at any time from within the app settings.
  • Complete Deletion: Clearing the Application's data in Android Settings (Settings > Apps > Local Attendance > Storage > Clear Data) or uninstalling the app permanently and irreversibly deletes all databases, vector caches, and session history from your device.

6. Third-Party Sharing & Analytics

Local Attendance does not contain:

  • Advertising networks or ad identifiers (GAID).
  • Third-party analytics or crash reporting SDKs that track user behavior.
  • Data brokers or tracking beacons.

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade any user information.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Educational Compliance)

Local Attendance is designed for use by schools, universities, instructors, and educational institutions. Because the Application does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on remote servers, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA).

Institutions and instructors maintain sole custody and ownership of their students' records on their respective devices.

8. Security of Your Data

Your data is protected by the Android sandbox environment and device-level hardware encryption. We recommend enabling a device lock screen (PIN, password, or biometrics) to prevent unauthorized physical access to the device hosting the attendance database.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be posted to this website (localattendance.evanferrao.com) with an updated revision date.
